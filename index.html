About

Doctoral Candidate & Researcher

Swarup Kumar Subudhi is a Ph.D. candidate and Graduate Research Assistant in Mechanical Engineering at the University of Maryland, College Park, specializing in fluid mechanics, soft magnetic materials, and printed electronics research. He holds a B.Tech in Mechanical Engineering from the National Institute of Technology, Rourkela and brings four years of industrial experience from Tata Motors. His work includes developing printable soft magnetic films, experimental nanoscale fluid dynamics, and published peer-reviewed research. He has presented at academic symposia and contributed to multidisciplinary materials science innovations, positioning him for advanced R&D roles in U.S. engineering and technology sectors.
