About

Doctoral Candidate & Researcher

I received my Bachelor’s degree in Mechanical Engineering in 2016 from National Institute of Technology, Rourkela. Then I worked at Tata Motors Ltd. where I pursued my interests in the automotive industry. Learned a lot both technically and professioanlly about dealing with solving a problem and the stakeholders associated with it. Machine design is my forte from my education and industrial experience. To further my education, I joined SMIEL Lab by Dr. Siddhartha Das as an MS student in Spring 2021. After a year, I converted to a Ph.D. student in Spring 2022. I became a Doctoral Candidate in Nov 2024 and aiming to finish my PhD by April 2026.

My research is two-pronged; while (i) one is towards fundamentals of magnetohydrodynamics and their applications in soft-robotics or targeted drug-delivery; (ii) the other is towards fine-printing of electronics components using DIW methods and subsequently reliability testing of printed electronics.
I am currently interested in career roles such as: Mechanical Engineer II, Mid-Level Mechanical Engineer, Product Design Engineer, Research Scientist, etc.
