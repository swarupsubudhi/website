Six-Sigma Projects to reduce Warranty Failures



1. Gearbox Bearing Failures

Project Title: Reduction in Warranty Failure in Gearbox due to Bearing Failures by 50%

Business Case/ Justification for PRoject Selection:
We aim to be most admired by all the stake holders including our customers. IPTV complaints hinder our mission. IPTV will in the long run reduce our market share that currently stands at 43%. Bearing Failure is one of the High Cost of Service Defects.



2. Gearbox Oil Leakage Failures

Project Title: All Oil Leakage Complaints : Reduction in Oil Leakage failures in Gearbox by 60%

Business Case/ Justification for PRoject Selection:
Gearbox oil leakage leads to functional failure & gearbox seizure in the long run and leads to vehicle downtime, thus bringing Customer dissatisfaction. It also increases Cost of Ownership.

Outcomes:

  • Gearbox Warranty Failures occuring due to bearing failures/issues reduced by 50% (in terms of Expense per Vehicle in warranty)

  • Expense per Vehicle due to gearbox Oil Leakage Failures reduced by upto 50-60% in 3MIS, 6MIS vehicles. Data for 12MIS & 24MIS failures is unavailable to me for validation
